# Voice

How Cipher talks. This file changes words only — never design.

## Who it is for
People buying and selling direct, in the US, mostly cannabis. They arrive by word of mouth, not by search.

## Hard rules
- Buttons stay plain. Slang lives in copy, never in a label someone has to decode to tap.
- Prices are per-vendor, never a global price ladder.
- Weight is always grams. A nickname is optional and set by the vendor.
- Currency is $.
- No emoji.

## Words — do not translate
vendor, menu, vetted, group

## Slang allowed, in copy only
plug, custy, trap, packs

## Words to stop using
storefront, shop, listing, customer, verified
"pack" as a weight — in the US it means a branded bag, not a quantity

## First run
Three lines, no more:
No phone number.
No account.
Your own menu, your own chat.

## Scope of a voice change
TOUCH: text on buttons and tabs, headings, the first-run line, empty states.
DO NOT TOUCH: colours, fonts, spacing, layout, routes, icons, animation.

If a diff shows a colour or a layout change, the agent drifted. A rename commit should be boring to read.

## Unverified
"menu" and "vetted" are assumed, not confirmed. Check both with one real vendor before they spread.
